Privacy policy

What we collect, who sees it and how to have it removed.

Effective 11 November 2026. This site is run by Eye To Ad Media in Denver, Colorado (zach@eyetoad.com).

What we collect

Only what you type into the quote form: name, phone, optional email, the places you are moving between, home size, a target month, and the consent text you agreed to with a time stamp. The form also records which page it was sent from. We do not run advertising trackers, analytics scripts or third-party fonts here.

Who sees it

A person at Eye To Ad Media reads each request. We then pass it to up to three moving companies we have vetted so they can contact you. They are independent businesses and handle your details under their own policies. We do not sell your details to anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep requests and proof of consent only as long as needed to run the service and answer disputes, then delete them.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to end texts.
  • Email zach@eyetoad.com to see, correct or delete your details.
  • Colorado residents may have rights under the Colorado Privacy Act; email us and we will respond.

Security

The form sends over an encrypted connection and screens for automated spam. No system is perfectly safe, so send only what the form asks for.
